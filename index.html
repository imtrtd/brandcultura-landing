Sound is organized vibration. So is the tattoo machine. The only difference is the medium: air or skin. Both turn frequency into permanent form.

Spektrogramm als Design-BriefSpectrogram as design brief

Wir lesen das Spektrogramm nicht als Dekoration, sondern als Bauplan. Jede Frequenzzone wird zur Design-Entscheidung: Dichte, Linie, Rhythmus, Leerraum.We don't treat the spectrogram as decoration, but as a blueprint. Every frequency zone becomes a design decision: density, line, rhythm, empty space.
